Free Online Tools for Everyday Tasks

ToolNest provides simple and free online tools for students, developers, writers, bloggers and everyday users. Use our text utilities, calculators and developer tools without creating an account.

1. Word & Character Counter

Count words, characters, sentences and paragraphs with our free online word counter. It is useful for students, writers, bloggers and professionals checking text length.

How to use

  1. Open the Word Counter.
  2. Paste or type your text.
  3. Click Count Text to see the results.

Example

Input: Welcome to ToolNest.

Result: 3 words and 20 characters including spaces.

Is this word counter free?

Yes. It is free to use without registration.

Does it count spaces?

Yes. It displays character counts with and without spaces.

2. Text Case Converter

Convert text into uppercase, lowercase, title case or sentence case. This free text case converter helps you format text quickly.

How to use

  1. Enter or paste your text.
  2. Select the required case.
  3. Click Convert Text.

Example

Input: hello world

Uppercase: HELLO WORLD

Can I convert uppercase to lowercase?

Yes. Select lowercase from the conversion options.

3. Remove Duplicate Lines

Remove repeated lines from text, lists and copied data. This duplicate line remover helps organize names, records and other text.

How to use

  1. Paste your lines into the text box.
  2. Choose whether to ignore letter case.
  3. Click Remove Duplicates.

Example

Input: Apple, Mango, Apple, Banana

Output: Apple, Mango, Banana

Does it preserve the original order?

Yes. The first occurrence of each line is retained.

4. JSON Formatter

Format and validate JSON data with this free online JSON formatter. Developers can use it to make API responses and nested JSON objects easier to read.

How to use

  1. Paste JSON data.
  2. Choose Pretty Print or Minify.
  3. Click Format / Validate.

Example

Input: {"name":"Alex","age":20}

The formatter displays the JSON in a readable structure.

What is JSON?

JSON is a lightweight data format commonly used to exchange information between applications.

5. Discount Calculator

Calculate discount amounts and final sale prices using this free discount calculator. It is useful when shopping or comparing product offers.

How to use

  1. Enter the original price.
  2. Enter the discount percentage.
  3. Click Calculate Discount.

Example

Original price: ₹1,000

Discount: 20%

Final price: ₹800

How do I calculate a discount?

Discount = Original price × Discount percentage ÷ 100.

6. Remove Extra Spaces

Clean text by removing unnecessary spaces and extra blank lines. This tool helps make copied text easier to read and organize.

How to use

  1. Paste your text.
  2. Click Clean Spaces.
  3. Copy the cleaned result.
Does it remove extra spaces?

Yes. It reduces repeated spaces and trims unnecessary spaces around lines.

7. Find & Replace Text

Find specific words or phrases and replace them with new text. This free online tool is useful for editing and cleaning text.

How to use

  1. Paste your original text.
  2. Enter the text to find.
  3. Enter replacement text and click Find & Replace.

8. Sort Lines Alphabetically

Arrange lines in alphabetical order from A to Z or Z to A. Organize names, words and lists with this online alphabetical sorter.

How to use

  1. Paste your list.
  2. Select A to Z or Z to A.
  3. Choose whether to remove duplicate lines.
  4. Click Sort Lines.

9. Reverse Text

Reverse characters or change the order of words using this free online text reverser.

Example

Input: Hello

Reversed: olleH

10. Text to Slug Converter

Convert titles and phrases into clean, SEO-friendly URL slugs. Useful for bloggers, website owners and developers.

Example

Input: Best Free Online Tools

Output: best-free-online-tools

11. Percentage Calculator

Calculate percentages, find what percentage one number is of another and calculate percentage changes.

Example

20% of 500 = 100

12. Age Calculator

Calculate your age in years, months and days by entering your date of birth. The calculator uses the current date.

How to use

  1. Enter your date of birth.
  2. Click Calculate Age.
  3. View your age in years, months and days.

13. GST Calculator India

Calculate Goods and Services Tax (GST) in India. Find GST amounts and totals using common tax rates.

Example

Price before GST: ₹1,000

GST at 18%: ₹180

Total price: ₹1,180

14. BMI Calculator

Calculate Body Mass Index using height and weight. This calculator provides a basic BMI estimate for adults. BMI is a screening measure, not a medical diagnosis.

Formula

BMI = Weight in kilograms ÷ (Height in metres × Height in metres).

15. QR Code Generator

Create QR codes from website links or text. Scan the generated QR code using a compatible phone or QR scanner.

How to use

  1. Enter a URL or text.
  2. Click Generate QR Code.
  3. Scan the generated code to test it.

Privacy note: QR generation uses an external QR service. The text or URL you enter is sent to that service to create the image.
