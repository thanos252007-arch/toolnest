Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Overview

ToolNest is a collection of browser-based utilities. We aim to collect as little information as reasonably necessary to operate the website.

Tool inputs

Most tools process text and numbers locally in your browser. ToolNest does not intentionally send those inputs to its own server when you use those tools.

QR Code Generator

The QR tool uses api.qrserver.com. The text or URL you enter is sent to that external service to create the image. The provider may process it under its own privacy practices. Do not enter confidential information.

Hosting

ToolNest is hosted on Cloudflare infrastructure. Hosting providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details and security logs to deliver and protect the website.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This version does not intentionally include its own analytics or advertising scripts. If analytics, advertising, accounts or forms are added, this policy should be updated to describe their data practices. Third-party services may use their own technologies under their policies.

External links

We do not control third-party websites. Review their policies before sharing information.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the site changes. For privacy questions, use the ToolNest GitHub Issues page.
